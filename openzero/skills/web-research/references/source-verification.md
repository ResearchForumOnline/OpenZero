# Source verification

Use this checklist when a conclusion depends on current or contested evidence:

1. Confirm the source identity, canonical URL, publication date, and relevant version.
2. Prefer an original artifact over a page summarizing it.
3. Check whether the source supports the exact claim rather than a nearby claim.
4. Compare event dates separately from article publication dates.
5. Record disagreement instead of averaging incompatible claims.
6. Quote sparingly and preserve context.
7. Mark calculations or synthesis as inference.

For security, medical, legal, or financial claims, require authoritative sources and clearly state that general research is not a substitute for a qualified professional.
